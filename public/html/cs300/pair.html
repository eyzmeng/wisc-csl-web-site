REGARDING EMAILS: I do NOT use Outlook for email communications and would advise you not to either. This is my opinion, but I think the Outlook-driven style of "top-posting" (replying on top of a copy of the original message in its entirety) is objectively bad for driving quaality conversations. It also happens to be the opinion of essentially everyone who are old enough to have communicated over email/Usenet in a time before Outlook. Most of the time I do believe any technology is merely a means to an end (for example, any IDE you use to code is fine), but this is an exception I willfully made because bad mainstream mail clients actively work AGAINST the end --- that being to have a meaningful, critical conversation that doesn't vaguely address the questions and points made by one another. (Sadly, this isn't a "modern" issue: they have always been that way since they came into existence some decades ago. It doesn't take much to encourage the practice of "bottom-posting", i.e. quoting the context that your recipient actually NEEDS, yet they simply DON'T.)

If you DO decide to use Outlook, kindly delete the horribly formatted "full message" at the bottom of your reply. I do not blame you if you don't, but if you can I'd greatly appreciate it. When you DO need to quote my sentences, precede the quote with a GREATER-THAN SIGN ">".
