How to turn in my Wiscard

Wow! If you are redirected to here, it seems that I lost my Wiscard again!

(seriously though how did i do that again)

But fortunately this time, I am prepared for this type of extremely-unlikely-but-not-impossible situation. I figured a name tag wouldn’t fit my contact information so I left you a link that brings you here. (The fact that you are here... hopefully means that you followed the link on my new Wiscard card holder with a lanyard that says “Chadbourne Residential College”, and not... that you went to pages.cs.wisc.edu and found this page listed in the index. I hope. :/)

Still with me? Cool! Here’s what I’m gonna need you to do. Read thoroughly if you can, but TL;DR: refrain from posting to social media publicly, and email me (the first bullet point should be tell you enough).

  • Email me or call me if you want to let me know: I should have written on the front of the card holder, but otherwise use my information in the directory. I check emails all the time. Chances are I will not miss them.
  • Please refrain from posting to social media platforms (Snapchat, Instagram, Twitter, etc.) as (1) I do not check those very often and (2) I will be very mad if you make a public post and do not blur out my face. (You can take a photo of my card, but keep it to yourself, and only show it to people you know. I hope you understand that names and photos are private things, even if they are presented as an ID....) You are more than welcome to blur out the whole picture frame -- that will even make me like you, in fact :)
    • And just to be clear (if you must post somewhere and are confident that it is visible to just a select group of people (like I would prefer that to not be the entirety of Class of 2028? But it’s better than, say, the whole world)), I am okay with my names visible (Ethan or Yizheng). I will be slightly concerned with my Campus ID visible (that is the 10-digit ID starting with 908), and very much so with the barcode on the back (which seems to be able to perform transactions on my behalf -- but I can freeze the card on my end so I can probably figure something out if you did post it). But what I will not tolerate is my face -- I explicitly forbid that and that alone, period.
    • For your information, the first (and last) time I lost my card the person who found it turned it in to a residental hall desk. Chances are you aren’t new to the school, so I believe you can find a front desk or a lost-and-found center of sorts -- whichever is closer to you. Again, if you want to let me know or ask me if I am near you, email me, and try your best not to post to social media.
  • You are awesome. If you read to this point and did something to return my card to me, I owe you a favor. Losing my card is fully my responsibility, and the fact that you stepped in to help shows that you are someone who cares -- and I will never put down someone who cares. If you want I can invite you to a meal, or help you with homework problems (I am moderately good at math and computer science and mediocre at everything else). Or if you just want a personal thank you...... but well. I’ll save it until we meet or when you send an email to me, so I can properly thank you there instead of here. :)

Thank you for reading, and I wish you the best!
